# SEC FOIA request — SR-TAD-76-1 Exhibits 2–3 and participant response

Please provide electronic copies of the following specifically identified records from SR-TAD-76-1:

- Exhibit 2, TAD Depository Corporation's January 7, 1976 letter soliciting participant comments on its proposed fee changes;
- the single written participant response received in response to that solicitation, including any attachment, cover correspondence, or other record identifying the respondent; and
- Exhibit 3, containing the additional comments TAD obtained through telephone and personal contacts.

This request is limited to these identified SR-TAD-76-1 records rather than the complete SR-TAD filing series. If the records are maintained only on microfiche, microfilm, retired paper, Federal Records Center/offsite storage, or legacy Public Reference Room holdings, please search those holdings and provide the best available reproduction.

## Sources identifying the records

The Commission's SR-TAD-76-1 notice states that TAD solicited participant comments through its January 7, 1976 letter, identified as Exhibit 2; received only one written response; and obtained additional telephone and personal comments that were contained in Exhibit 3. See [Securities Exchange Act Release No. 34-12199, 41 FR 11904 (Mar. 22, 1976)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-1976-03-22/pdf/FR-1976-03-22.pdf). The SEC News Digest also identifies SR-TAD-76-1 as TAD's proposed fee-schedule amendment. See [SEC News Digest, Mar. 15, 1976](https://www.sec.gov/news/digest/1976/dig031576.pdf).

## Fee category / waiver

Consistent with the SEC's recent classification of WhyDRS as a Media requester, these records are sought for source-linked public research and editorial analysis and will be disseminated freely to the public, not for a commercial use. I request treatment as a representative of the news media under 17 CFR § 200.80(g)(2)(vi) and a waiver or reduction of fees under 5 USC § 552(a)(4)(A)(iii) and 17 CFR § 200.80(g)(12), because the records document participant reactions to TAD's operations and fee structure during development of the national clearance and settlement system.
