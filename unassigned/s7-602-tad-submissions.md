# SEC FOIA request — TAD submission and follow-ups in File No. S7-602

Please provide electronic copies of all materials submitted by or on behalf of TAD Depository Corporation in SEC File No. S7-602 concerning the National Market Advisory Board's work on a national market system, including:

- TAD Depository Corporation's July 15, 1976 written submission;
- any TAD comments, amplifications, replies to comments of others, supplemental submissions, exhibits, attachments, or transmittal correspondence in the same file; and
- any Commission or National Market Advisory Board cover sheets, indexes, routing memoranda, or correspondence necessary to identify and place those TAD materials in context.

Please search the complete historical public file, including microfiche, microfilm, retired-paper, Federal Records Center/offsite, and legacy Public Reference Room holdings.

## Sources identifying the records

The National Market Advisory Board's final report lists "TAD Depository Corp. — 7/15/76" among written submissions and explains that submissions were maintained in the Commission's public-reference File No. S7-602. The report also notes that the Board received additional comments, amplifications, and replies to comments of others. See [Report of the National Market Advisory Board (1976)](https://www.sechistorical.org/collection/papers/1970/1976_1231_NMAB_Report_Con.pdf).

## Fee category / waiver

Consistent with the SEC's recent classification of WhyDRS as a Media requester, these records are sought for source-linked public research and editorial analysis and will be disseminated freely to the public, not for a commercial use. I request treatment as a representative of the news media under 17 CFR § 200.80(g)(2)(vi) and a waiver or reduction of fees under 5 USC § 552(a)(4)(A)(iii) and 17 CFR § 200.80(g)(12), because the records bear on the Commission's contemporaneous development of national-market and clearance-and-settlement policy.
