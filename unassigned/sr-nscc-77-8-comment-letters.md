# SEC FOIA request — SR-NSCC-77-8 comment letters

Please provide electronic copies of all comment letters contained in SEC File No. SR-NSCC-77-8, including:

- the five written comment letters annexed as Exhibit 2 to NSCC’s filing in response to its June 16, 1977 participant solicitation, including the letter from TAD Depository Corporation; and
- the separate comment letter received by the Commission after publication of the proposed rule change.

Please also include any cover sheet, exhibit index, or transmittal correspondence needed to identify the authors and dates of those letters. I am seeking the existing public-file records, not a broader search for substantive communications outside SR-NSCC-77-8. If the records are maintained only on microfiche, microfilm, retired paper, or offsite storage, please search those holdings.

## Sources identifying the records

The September 27, 1977 notice states that four NSCC participants and one registered clearing agency submitted written comments and that the five letters were annexed as Exhibit 2. Its summary includes requests for sponsored TAD accounts and TAD-specific fee treatment, as well as an allegation that parts of NSCC’s fee schedule constituted “predatory pricing.” See [Securities Exchange Act Release No. 34-13968, 42 FR 49534, 49536 (Sept. 27, 1977)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-1977-09-27/pdf/FR-1977-09-27.pdf).

The Commission’s October 1978 approval order identifies TAD Depository Corporation by name as a commenter, summarizes TAD’s requested treatment for its NSCC interface, states that the Commission separately received one comment letter, and says that all comment letters were placed in File No. SR-NSCC-77-8 and were “available to the public.” See [Securities Exchange Act Release No. 34-15222, 43 FR 47620, 47624 (Oct. 16, 1978)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-1978-10-16/pdf/FR-1978-10-16.pdf).

## Fee category / waiver

Consistent with the SEC’s recent classification of WhyDRS as a Media requester, these records are sought for source-linked public research and editorial analysis and will be disseminated freely to the public, not for a commercial use. I request treatment as a representative of the news media under 17 CFR § 200.80(g)(2)(vi) and, because the records illuminate SEC oversight of competition and access within the national clearance and settlement system, a waiver or reduction of fees under 5 USC § 552(a)(4)(A)(iii) and 17 CFR § 200.80(g)(12).
