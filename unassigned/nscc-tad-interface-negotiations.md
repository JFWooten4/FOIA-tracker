# SEC FOIA request — NSCC–TAD interface negotiations and linking records

Please provide electronic copies of all Commission records concerning negotiations, proposals, agreements, or attempted implementation of a link or interface between National Securities Clearing Corporation (NSCC) and TAD Depository Corporation (TAD), including:

- records concerning meetings between NSCC and TAD;
- the proposed method of linking that NSCC represented in May 1977 it had developed and intended to provide to TAD for review;
- any TAD response, comments, counterproposal, requested changes, acceptance, rejection, or follow-up correspondence;
- draft or final interface agreements, operating agreements, technical specifications, procedures, data formats, implementation schedules, fee schedules, sponsored-account arrangements, and testing or readiness materials;
- Commission correspondence, memoranda of meetings or telephone calls, staff notes, transmittal letters, or internal routing records concerning the NSCC–TAD link;
- records concerning the Commission's requirement in Exchange Act Release No. 34-13163 that NSCC establish an appropriate link with TAD;
- records concerning later representations that an NSCC–TAD agreement had been reached; and
- records concerning why the interface was delayed, not completed, or not operating before TAD suspended operations in 1978.

Please search File No. 600-15 and any related NSCC, TAD, clearing-agency-registration, SRO-rule-filing, Public Reference Room, microfiche, microfilm, retired-paper, or offsite holdings. This request does not assume that every bilateral NSCC–TAD document was filed with the Commission; it seeks those records that came into Commission custody.

## Sources identifying the records

A May 1977 Commission notice reports NSCC's representation that it had met with TAD, developed an approach to the required link, and expected to provide TAD a proposed method of linking for review. See [Federal Register, May 18, 1977](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-1977-05-18/pdf/FR-1977-05-18.pdf).

The Commission's January 1977 NSCC registration order required an appropriate link with TAD. See [42 FR 3916, 3938 (Jan. 21, 1977)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-1977-01-21/pdf/FR-1977-01-21.pdf). TAD's later SR-TAD-77-2 filing states that an NSCC interface was contemplated, while SR-TAD-78-1 states that TAD's repeated efforts to establish interfaces with NSCC and DTC had been frustrated. See [42 FR 41204 (Aug. 15, 1977)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-1977-08-15/pdf/FR-1977-08-15.pdf) and [43 FR 12979 (Mar. 28, 1978)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-1978-03-28/pdf/FR-1978-03-28.pdf).

## Fee category / waiver

Consistent with the SEC's recent classification of WhyDRS as a Media requester, these records are sought for source-linked public research and editorial analysis and will be disseminated freely to the public, not for a commercial use. I request treatment as a representative of the news media under 17 CFR § 200.80(g)(2)(vi) and a waiver or reduction of fees under 5 USC § 552(a)(4)(A)(iii) and 17 CFR § 200.80(g)(12), because the records concern implementation of Commission-mandated inter-clearing-agency access and competition within the national clearance and settlement system.
