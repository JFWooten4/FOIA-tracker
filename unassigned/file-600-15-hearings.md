# SEC FOIA request — File No. 600-15 national clearance and settlement hearings

Please provide electronic copies of the complete public hearing record associated with SEC File No. 600-15 concerning establishment of a national clearance and settlement system, including:

- transcripts of the March 7–10, 1978 public hearings;
- transcripts of the April 19–20, 1978 reconvened public hearings;
- all prepared statements, exhibits, attachments, supplemental statements, and written comments submitted for either hearing; and
- in particular, all testimony, prepared statements, exhibits, supplemental submissions, and correspondence submitted by or on behalf of the Bradford Group, Bradford National Corporation, Bradford Securities Processing Services, Bradford National Clearing Corporation, and TAD Depository Corporation.

Please include transcript volumes and indexes even if individual submissions are duplicated elsewhere in File No. 600-15. If these public records are maintained only on microfiche, microfilm, retired paper, Federal Records Center/offsite storage, or legacy Public Reference Room holdings, please search those holdings and provide the best available reproduction.

## Sources identifying the records

The Commission's January 25, 1978 notice announced public hearings beginning March 7, 1978 on the development of a national clearance and settlement system, directed prepared witnesses to furnish 25 copies of their statements, invited written submissions, and required reference to File No. 600-15. See [Securities Exchange Act Release No. 34-14411, 43 FR 4295 (Feb. 1, 1978)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-1978-02-01/pdf/FR-1978-02-01.pdf) and [SEC News Digest, Jan. 25, 1978](https://www.sec.gov/news/digest/1978/dig012578.pdf).

The Commission's March 3, 1978 witness schedule identifies a Bradford Group consisting of Bradford National Corporation, Bradford Securities Processing Services, Bradford National Clearing Corporation, and TAD Depository Corporation, scheduled for March 7–8. See [SEC News Digest, Mar. 3, 1978](https://www.sec.gov/news/digest/1978/dig030378.pdf).

When the Commission reconvened the hearings in April, it expressly stated that transcripts of the March hearings were available at the Commission's Public Reference Room and, upon request, at its regional offices. The notice also invited additional written comments concerning clearing-agency interfaces, required reference to File No. 600-15, and stated that all communications would be placed in that file and available for public inspection. See [43 FR 16269, 16270 (Apr. 17, 1978)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-1978-04-17/pdf/FR-1978-04-17.pdf).

## Fee category / waiver

Consistent with the SEC's recent classification of WhyDRS as a Media requester, these records are sought for source-linked public research and editorial analysis and will be disseminated freely to the public, not for a commercial use. I request treatment as a representative of the news media under 17 CFR § 200.80(g)(2)(vi) and a waiver or reduction of fees under 5 USC § 552(a)(4)(A)(iii) and 17 CFR § 200.80(g)(12), because this historical public hearing record bears directly on the Commission's development and oversight of the national clearance and settlement system.
