# SEC FOIA request — SR-TAD-76-1 and TAD historical correspondence

Please provide electronic copies of the following records:

- SR-TAD-76-1 Exhibit 2, TAD Depository Corporation’s January 7, 1976 letter soliciting participant comments;
- SR-TAD-76-1 Exhibit 3 and the single written participant response referenced in the filing, including any attachment, cover correspondence, or other record identifying the respondent;
- the October 21, 1980 letter from Mr. Moskowitz to Commission staff in which TAD indicated its desire to withdraw its clearing-agency registration; and
- any later written reaffirmation by BNC counsel of the October 21, 1980 letter’s representations, including any correspondence concerning the four settled dividend claims and the three later transfer-agent claims described by the Commission in Release No. 34-18583.

Please search the relevant SR-TAD-76-1 records and TAD registration File No. 600-13. If the records are maintained only on microfiche, microfilm, retired paper, or offsite storage, please search those holdings.

## Sources identifying the records

The March 22, 1976 SR-TAD-76-1 notice states that TAD solicited participant comments through its January 7, 1976 letter (Exhibit 2), received only one written response, and obtained further telephone and personal comments that were contained in Exhibit 3. See [Securities Exchange Act Release No. 34-12199, 41 FR 11904 (Mar. 22, 1976)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-1976-03-22/pdf/FR-1976-03-22.pdf). The SEC News Digest also identifies SR-TAD-76-1 as TAD’s proposed fee-schedule amendment. See [SEC News Digest, Mar. 15, 1976](https://www.sec.gov/news/digest/1976/dig031576.pdf).

The Commission’s 1982 termination order states that an October 21, 1980 letter from Mr. Moskowitz represented that TAD had been inactive since April 1, 1978 and would not be reactivated; it also states that BNC counsel later reaffirmed those representations. The order further describes four dividend claims addressed in the correspondence and three later transfer-agent claims. See [Securities Exchange Act Release No. 34-18583, 47 FR 13262–63 (Mar. 29, 1982), File Nos. 600-2 and 600-13](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-1982-03-29/pdf/FR-1982-03-29.pdf).

## Fee category / waiver

Consistent with the SEC’s recent classification of WhyDRS as a Media requester, these records are sought for source-linked public research and editorial analysis and will be disseminated freely to the public, not for a commercial use. I request treatment as a representative of the news media under 17 CFR § 200.80(g)(2)(vi) and, because the records illuminate SEC oversight of registered clearing agencies and the development of the national clearance and settlement system, a waiver or reduction of fees under 5 USC § 552(a)(4)(A)(iii) and 17 CFR § 200.80(g)(12).
