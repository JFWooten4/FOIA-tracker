# SEC FOIA request — 1975 NCC–TAD interface filing package

Please provide electronic copies of the complete July 22, 1975 filing package concerning implementation of the depository interface between National Clearing Corporation (NCC) and TAD Depository Corporation (TAD), including:

- all TAD rules filed by the Boston Stock Exchange, PBW Stock Exchange, Inc., and Midwest Stock Exchange, Inc.;
- all procedures, reports, forms, operating instructions, technical specifications, agreements, schedules, and exhibits submitted by NCC;
- all amendments, supplements, transmittal letters, correspondence, meeting memoranda, and Commission staff communications concerning the filing or implementation of the interface; and
- the complete underlying records associated with Exchange Act Release Nos. 34-11586, 34-11587, 34-11588, and 34-11589.

Please search any historical SRO-rule-filing, Public Reference Room, microfiche, microfilm, retired-paper, or offsite holdings where the July 22, 1975 submissions may be maintained.

## Sources identifying the records

The SEC News Digest states that the Boston, PBW, and Midwest exchanges filed new TAD rules relating to implementation of the NCC–TAD interface, while NCC submitted procedures, reports, and forms for the same interface. It states that the materials were filed with the Commission on July 22, 1975 and put into effect effective July 31, 1975. See [SEC News Digest, Aug. 14, 1975](https://www.sec.gov/news/digest/1975/dig081475.pdf).

The Federal Register notice likewise describes the NCC–TAD interface and the Commission's action on the filed rules and procedures. See [40 FR 36179 (Aug. 19, 1975)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-1975-08-19/pdf/FR-1975-08-19.pdf).

## Fee category / waiver

Consistent with the SEC's recent classification of WhyDRS as a Media requester, these records are sought for source-linked public research and editorial analysis and will be disseminated freely to the public, not for a commercial use. I request treatment as a representative of the news media under 17 CFR § 200.80(g)(2)(vi) and a waiver or reduction of fees under 5 USC § 552(a)(4)(A)(iii) and 17 CFR § 200.80(g)(12), because the records document an early inter-clearing-agency interface used in the development of the national clearance and settlement system.
