# SEC FOIA request — complete SR-TAD rule-filing series

Please provide electronic copies of the complete underlying SEC rule-filing records for every proposed rule change filed by TAD Depository Corporation under Section 19(b), including at minimum SR-TAD-76-1, SR-TAD-76-2, SR-TAD-77-1, SR-TAD-77-2, and SR-TAD-78-1.

For each file, please include the original filing, all amendments and supplements, every exhibit and attachment, participant notices or solicitations, written responses or comments received by TAD or the Commission, transmittal correspondence, agreements, procedures, forms, technical specifications, implementation materials, and Commission staff correspondence maintained with the filing.

In particular, please include any materials concerning:

- the SR-TAD-76-1 fee proposal, January 7, 1976 participant solicitation, Exhibit 3, and the single written participant response;
- the SR-TAD-76-2 pledged-securities service;
- the SR-TAD-77-1 TAD–DTC interface account, including any interface agreement or technical/operational materials;
- the SR-TAD-77-2 release of TAD & Co. nominee certificates in connection with contemplated NSCC and DTC interfaces; and
- the SR-TAD-78-1 suspension of operations, including supporting records concerning TAD's stated repeated efforts to establish NSCC and DTC interfaces and the impediments it said prevented it from becoming a full-service competing depository.

Please search historical SRO-rule-filing, microfiche, microfilm, retired-paper, Federal Records Center/offsite, and legacy Public Reference Room holdings for the complete files.

## Fee category / waiver

Consistent with the SEC's recent classification of WhyDRS as a Media requester, these records are sought for source-linked public research and editorial analysis and will be disseminated freely to the public, not for a commercial use. I request treatment as a representative of the news media under 17 CFR § 200.80(g)(2)(vi) and a waiver or reduction of fees under 5 USC § 552(a)(4)(A)(iii) and 17 CFR § 200.80(g)(12), because the filings are primary public records of TAD's regulated services, interfaces, and eventual suspension of operations.
