# SEC FOIA request — October 21, 1980 TAD withdrawal letter

Please provide electronic copies of the following specifically identified records from TAD Depository Corporation's clearing-agency registration File No. 600-13:

- the October 21, 1980 letter from Mr. Moskowitz to Commission staff representing that TAD had been inactive since April 1, 1978, desired to withdraw its clearing-agency registration, and would not be reactivated; and
- the later written communication from Bradford National Corporation counsel reaffirming the representations in the October 21, 1980 letter.

Please include any attachments or transmittal material belonging to those two communications. This request is limited to the identified withdrawal correspondence rather than the complete File No. 600-13 record. If the records are maintained only on microfiche, microfilm, retired paper, Federal Records Center/offsite storage, or legacy Public Reference Room holdings, please search those holdings and provide the best available reproduction.

## Sources identifying the records

The Commission's 1982 termination order states that an October 21, 1980 letter from Mr. Moskowitz represented that TAD had been inactive since April 1, 1978, desired withdrawal of its registration, and would not be reactivated. The order further states that counsel for Bradford National Corporation subsequently reaffirmed those representations. See [Securities Exchange Act Release No. 34-18583, 47 FR 13262–63 (Mar. 29, 1982), File Nos. 600-2 and 600-13](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-1982-03-29/pdf/FR-1982-03-29.pdf).

## Fee category / waiver

Consistent with the SEC's recent classification of WhyDRS as a Media requester, these records are sought for source-linked public research and editorial analysis and will be disseminated freely to the public, not for a commercial use. I request treatment as a representative of the news media under 17 CFR § 200.80(g)(2)(vi) and a waiver or reduction of fees under 5 USC § 552(a)(4)(A)(iii) and 17 CFR § 200.80(g)(12), because the records document the withdrawal and termination of a registered clearing agency during development of the national clearance and settlement system.
