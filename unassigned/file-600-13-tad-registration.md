# SEC FOIA request — complete TAD clearing-agency registration File No. 600-13

Please provide electronic copies of the complete public clearing-agency registration record for TAD Depository Corporation in SEC File No. 600-13, including:

- TAD's original Form CA-1 registration application and every amendment;
- every schedule, exhibit, attachment, rule, agreement, financial or operational submission, and other document incorporated into or supplementing the application;
- Commission deficiency letters, requests for information, staff correspondence, TAD responses, transmittal correspondence, and memoranda of meetings or conversations maintained in the public file;
- all records connected with the September 1976 registration proceeding and subsequent extensions of TAD's temporary registration;
- all public comments or submissions concerning TAD's registration;
- all records concerning TAD's suspension of operations, inactive status, and request to withdraw; and
- the complete correspondence and supporting records underlying Exchange Act Release No. 34-18583, including the October 21, 1980 Moskowitz letter, later reaffirmations by Bradford National Corporation counsel, and materials concerning the dividend and transfer-agent claims described by the Commission.

This request seeks the complete pre-EDGAR public registration file, rather than only particular correspondence already identified. If the file survives only in microfiche, microfilm, retired paper, Federal Records Center/offsite storage, or legacy Public Reference Room holdings, please search those holdings and provide the best available reproduction.

## Sources identifying the records

The Commission's September 1976 institution of registration proceedings expressly identifies TAD Depository Corporation as File No. 600-13. See [41 FR 38841 (Sept. 13, 1976)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-1976-09-13/pdf/FR-1976-09-13.pdf).

The Commission's 1982 termination order again identifies File No. 600-13, recounts TAD's temporary registration and registration proceeding, cites the October 21, 1980 withdrawal letter and later reaffirmation, and describes related claims. See [Exchange Act Release No. 34-18583, 47 FR 13262–63 (Mar. 29, 1982)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-1982-03-29/pdf/FR-1982-03-29.pdf).

## Fee category / waiver

Consistent with the SEC's recent classification of WhyDRS as a Media requester, these records are sought for source-linked public research and editorial analysis and will be disseminated freely to the public, not for a commercial use. I request treatment as a representative of the news media under 17 CFR § 200.80(g)(2)(vi) and a waiver or reduction of fees under 5 USC § 552(a)(4)(A)(iii) and 17 CFR § 200.80(g)(12), because the file is the primary Commission record of a registered clearing agency's formation, regulation, operation, and termination during development of the national clearance and settlement system.
